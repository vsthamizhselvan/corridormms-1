# CorridorKey 2.0 Desktop — Community Guidelines

## 1. Vision & Purpose

CorridorKey 2.0 Desktop exists to empower indie filmmakers, professional visual effects artists, content creators, and open-source software developers with state-of-the-art AI matting and VFX tools.

We are committed to building an open, friendly, collaborative community where users of all skill levels—from VFX students to senior technical directors—can ask questions, share workflows, and improve the tool.

---

## 2. Communication Channels

* **GitHub Issues:** Used exclusively for tracking verified bugs, technical defects, installation errors, and structured feature proposals.
* **GitHub Discussions (when enabled):** The best place to share footage tests, discuss keying workflows, seek hardware recommendations, and share community presets.
* **Pull Requests:** Technical reviews for bug fixes, performance optimizations, and documentation improvements.

---

## 3. Guiding Principles

1. **Be Constructive & Kind:** Treat fellow creators with respect. Critique code and workflows, never individuals.
2. **Protect Sensitive Content:** Never share client footage, private NDA assets, or proprietary material in public issues or pull requests.
3. **Respect Upstream Licenses:** Honor the Corridor Key Licence Version 2.0 and the licenses of NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Google DeepMind foundation models.
4. **Reproducible Feedback:** When reporting an issue, always provide step-by-step instructions, media resolution, hardware specifications, and system logs.
